# Secret Objectives (multiplayer brainstorm)

Owner: Grok (Dave's multiplayer fun director). Status: **BRAINSTORM, nothing approved, no code.**
Started 4 Nov 2026 from Dave's notes. Keep adding ideas here.

## Dave's vision
- For tables of 3, 4, 5 or 6 players, the fun on top of the game is **secret objective cards**.
- **Every other round** each player is *dealt* a secret objective. It isn't optional: you just get it.
- On the other rounds it stays like today: you can choose to make a bet and risk gold on a personal objective.
- You play the round with no idea what anyone else's card says.
- The feel is Werewolf or Battlestar Galactica: friends on the same team, still winning together, but it's fun to trick each other a little.
- Dave's seed idea (one of ~20): two players get the same card, "Roll for the most gold this round and gain a bunch of gold." Neither knows the other has it. Whoever doesn't get a bunch of gold loses half their gold.

## Design rule (draft)
Secrets create small, funny betrayals inside a team that still wins together. No one is knocked out.
At the end of the round every card flips face up at once (the "reveal moment"), and that's where the laughing happens.

## Ideas

### Hidden rivals (two players unknowingly get linked cards)
- **Gold Rush:** two players get "Earn the most gold this round." The winner takes a big pile and the loser drops half their gold.
- **Glory Hound:** two players get "Land the killing blow." Suddenly everyone is holding back damage to steal the last hit.
- **Mirror:** two players get "Roll more swords than X," each naming the other.

### The quiet saboteur (fine for the team, bad for one person)
- **Grudge:** "Make sure Player X ends this round below half health" (block less for them, or pull aggro onto them).
- **Hoarder:** "Don't use any magic this round." No one knows why you're holding back.
- **Leech:** "Take more healing than anyone else."

### Bluff and accuse (guessing is part of the payout)
- **The Mole:** one random player gets "The party must lose at least 20 health this round." At round end everyone votes: a correct vote stops the payout, a wrong vote doubles it.
- **Whisper:** everyone gets a card, but only one is real. The rest say "Nothing, act like you have one." Free paranoia.
- **Wager on a friend:** "Bet that Player X takes the most damage." You profit by steering monsters toward them.

### Helpful secrets (so it's not all knives)
- **Guardian Angel:** "Keep Player X above half health." If they survive you both get paid, and they never knew.
- **Pact:** two players get "If you both finish without healing, you both gain 50."
- **Reveal-and-flip:** guess someone's objective out loud correctly and you steal their reward.

## Open questions
- How mean can it get? Can a secret hurt a friend's health, or only cost gold and bragging rights?
- Payout sizes and penalties (e.g. is "lose half your gold" too harsh in Act I?).
- How cards are dealt: fully random, or weighted so linked pairs show up often?
- Do solo and two-player games get a version, or is this 3+ only?
- Where the reveal shows on the phones in online play.
