## What was integrated

| Spoils (victory draft, online draft) | 3D portrait row | The card beside the decision: tap it for the large card; face strip; Take / Sell as before |
| Pack and Gear rows, the shelf, "Worth saving for" | picture row | Painted icon, name, metal, die, wear, face strip, a labelled gold figure ("Sell n" or "Store n"); tap the row for the large card; the buttons stay below the row |

New files: `js/g3/cards.js` (card data, the card, the icon, strip and chips; only reads), `css/cards.css`, `assets/cards/`, `js/previewtag.js`. Changed: `js/g3/scrui.js` (`weaponCard`, `shWeapon`, the envelope), `js/secrets.js`, `index.html` (stylesheet link). Prices, wear and inventory behaviour are untouched: the gold figure on a card is `sellValue` (or the price the shelf already holds, labelled Store, or `diamondSellValue` for a Diamond) read from the same functions the buttons use.

**Arrows** show the selected ammunition badge (`inst.ammo`, with the engine's own default) and the matching arrowhead faces on the card, strip and chips, for Normal, Piercing, Fire and Frost.

**Rendering never triggers wear.** `cards.js` imports only getters (`weaponFaces`, `weaponSize`, `battlesLeft`, `wearLabel`, `sellValue`, `diamondSellValue`). A unit test draws every card kind for a hero's pack, Diamond and hands and asserts the hero JSON is byte-identical afterwards; the browser run does the same after drawing spoils, the full-card pop-ups, the pack and the store (`noWear.unchanged: true` at both sizes). Bow and Arrows remain two items, each wearing once per fight through the existing path.

## The offered-bribe wording (secrets.js)

`rewardChips` is now exported, with a new `rewardTerms`, and the card no longer keeps its own copy. The old chip "Fail: pay G/2" did not say when. What `judge()` actually pays for an accepted offer: done and the team wins, **2G**; the team wins but it was not done, **nothing, and nothing is lost**; the team is defeated, **G/2, done or not, and G/2 more from every hero when every offered bribe at the table was taken** (the "crooks" clause, `secrets.js` `judge()`; there is at most one bribe a table, so accepting and being defeated costs the taker the full G in practice). The chips now read "Done and won: 2G gold", "Won, not done: nothing", "Team defeated: pay G/2, G/2 more if every bribe was taken", and the "More" sentence says the same. A test compares each figure with what `judge()` pays for done-and-won, won-not-done and team-defeated (done or not). Note: the handoff said defeat costs G/2 only; the crooks clause makes that incomplete, so the wording includes it.

For a Bonus Challenge (solo) the stake is lost whether the objective is missed or the fight is lost, so its "If not" row says exactly that.
