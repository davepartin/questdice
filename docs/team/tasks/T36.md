# T36 — Camp practice dummy

Dave (creator, morning of 6 November 2026) asked for a camp fireside practice dummy, like Minecraft Dungeons' training dummy: a wooden Renaissance-faire scarecrow by the campfire that you tap to practice rolls, see how big your numbers get, with unlimited magic, every attempt starting back at zero.

**Build:** `20261106pd1`. One PR; do not merge to main.

## Player experience

- At Camp, the painted dummy stands to the **right of the fire**; the hero miniature stays on the left. Label: **Practice**.
- Tap it to enter a one-round practice session against the dummy, using the current hero loadout (class, weapons, talents, learned magic).
- Magic is unlimited (`b.freeMagic`): every magic card is usable; costs are not deducted. The magic chip shows ∞.
- The dummy does not fight back, grant XP or loot, or change the campaign. It is not in `MONSTERS`.
- Colour counters (Attack, Pierce, Splash, Block, Heal, Gold, Magic generated) start at zero each attempt. Splash still shows its pink total against one dummy.
- After the player round (Use cards, Roll, Lock in): **Again** starts at zero, **Back to camp** leaves.
- Enter snapshots gold, HP/heal, magic, charges, consumables, items, XP and quest flags; exit restores them. Persist is skipped while `S.campPractice` is set.

## How practice is entered and reset

1. Camp (offline) renders `dummyFigure` in the fireside banner (`js/g3/scenery.js`). Online tables hide it.
2. Tap calls `startCampPractice()` in `js/ui.js`: snapshot the run, `newBattle` with `quest.campPractice`, skip the quest envelope, start the 3D table.
3. `Again` calls `E.resetPracticeRound` then `renderReset` (same table, counters and spent cards cleared).
4. `Back to camp` / battle menu calls `leaveCampPractice()`: stop the table, restore the snapshot, `showCamp`.

## Art

Official dummy painting converted to transparent WebP at `assets/miniatures/training-dummy.webp` (no JPEG in the repo). Used as the camp figure and the practice-target portrait. Camp banner `assets/bg/camp.webp` is unchanged; the dummy is composited like the hero miniature.

## Rules kept

Live card rules, prices, dice templates, Cleave copy, empty-socket marks, and real battle behaviour outside this mode are unchanged. Tutorial `S.practice` is a different mode (guided goblin fight).

## Limitations

- Headless Chromium on Linux, not Safari / not a real iPhone. Phone layout for camp tap-through (`pointer-events` on the sheet so the banner dummy can be tapped) should be checked on Dave's iPhone.
- Practice is one player round: Roll and Lock in still finish the attempt so the totals freeze; the dummy never takes a turn.
- Online camp hides the dummy (practice is local-only).
