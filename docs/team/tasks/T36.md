# T36 — Camp practice dummy

Dave (creator, morning of 6 November 2026) asked for a camp fireside practice dummy, like Minecraft Dungeons' training dummy: a wooden Renaissance-faire scarecrow by the campfire that you tap to practice rolls, see how big your numbers get, with unlimited magic, every attempt starting back at zero.

Live follow-up the same afternoon: Dave tried build `20261106pd1` on his iPhone and was stuck mid-practice. The bottom bar was only Lock In / Magic Powers / Free reroll. Recap and the hamburger were there, but nothing that said **Back to camp** until after Lock in.

**Build:** `20261106pd5` (follows `20261106pd4` on main via PR 56). One PR; do not merge to main.

Live iPhone follow-up on `20261106pd4`: Dave could not scroll any camp tab, and tapping the name/gold chip at the top granted 5,000 gold. Cause of the scroll bug: PR 54’s phone punch-through (`.cw-camp .sx-side { pointer-events: none }` with children `auto`) so the dummy could be tapped through the sheet. iPhone Safari will not pan a scroller that is itself `pointer-events: none`. Fix: the sheet scrolls normally again; a small transparent `.camp-dummy-hit` button sits over the dummy only. The 5,000-gold tap was a playtest helper on the gold chip in `heroChip` (Gemini, build `20261103g9`); it is gone from live. `?debug` `window.QD.grantGold()` remains for checks.

## Player experience

- At Camp, the painted dummy stands to the **right of the fire**; the hero miniature stays on the left. Label: **Practice**, sitting clearly **below** the dummy’s round dirt miniature base (not on the rim, not hanging off it).
- Tap it to enter a one-round practice session against the dummy, using the current hero loadout (class, weapons, talents, learned magic).
- Magic is unlimited (`b.freeMagic`): every magic card is usable; costs are not deducted. The magic chip shows ∞.
- The dummy does not fight back, grant XP or loot, or change the campaign. It is not in `MONSTERS`.
- Colour counters (Attack, Pierce, Splash, Block, Heal, Gold, Magic generated) start at zero each attempt. Splash still shows its pink total against one dummy.
- **Back to camp** sits in Recap's header slot on the ROUND I line (Dave's iPhone screenshot: Recap was there, no leave). Visible throughout practice: before rolling, after rolling or cards, and after Lock in. Recap is hidden in this mode so the exit is the labeled button, not the hamburger. After Lock in, **Again** (start at zero on the same table) and a second **Back to camp** stay on the result strip.
- Enter snapshots gold, HP/heal, magic, charges, consumables, items, XP and quest flags; exit restores them. Persist is skipped while `S.campPractice` is set. Practice totals, gold, heals and spent items are discarded.

## How practice is entered and reset

1. Camp (offline) renders `dummyFigure` in the fireside banner (`js/g3/scenery.js`). Online tables hide it.
2. Tap calls `startCampPractice()` in `js/ui.js`: snapshot the run, `newBattle` with `quest.campPractice`, skip the quest envelope, start the 3D table.
3. The HUD header shows `.b3-leave-camp` (**Back to camp**) in Recap's slot on the ROUND line for the whole session. Tap calls `leaveCampPractice()` — the same restore as the result-strip button and the battle-menu extra.
4. `Again` calls `E.resetPracticeRound` then `renderReset` (same table, counters and spent cards cleared).
5. Result-strip **Back to camp** / battle menu also call `leaveCampPractice()`: stop the table, restore the snapshot, `showCamp`.

## Rules kept

Live card rules, prices, dice templates, Cleave copy, empty-socket marks, and real battle behaviour outside this mode are unchanged. Tutorial `S.practice` is a different mode (guided goblin fight). Live fights do not show the header leave button.

## Limitations

- Headless Chromium on Linux, not Safari / not a real iPhone. Camp tab touch-scroll and dummy overlay should be checked on Dave's iPhone.
- Practice is one player round: Roll and Lock in still finish the attempt so the totals freeze; the dummy never takes a turn.
- Online camp hides the dummy (practice is local-only).
