## Charms and perks (Dave: "charms have been removed")

- **Charms are suspended, not deleted.** The 10 charms are still in `data.js` with their code in `engine.js`, but `TEAMWORK.noCharms` (set by `applyRelease`) gates every charm path: `hasCharm`, `takeCharm`, offers, the shop. Saved ownership is preserved so a reversal is possible. **Nobody should restore charms or propose charm-based fixes**; tools must keep `RELEASE=1`, which includes resting charms.
- **Perks:** 12 in `PERKS`: diamond, vitality, reserve, deepwell, quick, keen, ward, piercer, greed, grit, linebreaker, frugal. One perk choice per level gained. `perkPreview` shows the exact before/after number on the camp card.

## Levels: the cap and what each level gives

- **Cap:** `MAX_LEVEL = 20`. XP to the next level is `28 + 12 × (level − 1)`. Total XP to reach level 5 is 184, level 10 is 684, level 14 is 1,300, level 20 is 2,584.
- **Each level gives** +4 maximum health (`maxHpOf`) and 1 perk choice (`pendingPerks`).
- **Level gates:**
  - **Level 5:** the second big move in slot A for every class (Judgment, Rain of Arrows, Meteor, Avalanche, Finale; `unlock: 5`).
  - **Level 4:** the third potion slot (`POTION_BELT`, plus 90 gold).
  - Everything else (dice sizes, talent dice, weapons, power levels) is bought with gold at camp, not gated by level.
- **Where heroes actually get to** (released rules, smart player, normal, 15 solo campaigns, three per class, measured today):

  | End of | Level |
  |---|---|
  | Act I | 5–6 (mean 5.9) |
  | Act II | 10 |
  | Act III | 14 |

  Levels 15–20 are reached only by replaying or by future content. That matters for T03 (mixed levels): a "veteran" in Act III is about level 14.
- **Party strength:** `teamFactor` (a hero's measured strength, `heroStrength`) scales monsters by the company's real strength, not head count. A strong veteran therefore raises the monsters for the newcomer too. That is the existing system T03 should examine before inventing another.

## Known gaps in the measuring tools

- **The simulated players never:** turn the heart die (Heart Change), throw potions, share magic, or revive a friend. Those outlets read as zero in the tables, which is a gap, not evidence. T07 asks for Heart Change to be considered.
- **Partial-charge releases:** the smart bot releases a charge only when full, when hurt, or when a foe is low. The wise bot considers storing or releasing every round. T07 will check partial releases explicitly.
- **Fights within one campaign are not independent.** Use several campaigns and report error across campaigns (`tools/party.mjs` and `tools/pairs.mjs` now do).
- **Rule text:** the old "START HERE" state summary in `docs/PLAN.md` (10 October) is history. It predates the new weapons, charms resting, Heart to Heart, class rules, the 361 tests and the current stamp. This file is the current state.
- **Six-hero phone checks** are headless Chromium only; real-phone Safari checks are still Dave's.
