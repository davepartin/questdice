2. **Scope:** authorize a preview-only correction for returning **boss-tier nemeses**, applying the current act/step elite health basis to that enemy instead of inheriting the ordinary encounter multiplier. Preserve its wound fraction, grudge, bounty, quest identity and rewards. Do not broadly remove step-8 `fightHp` or rewrite all substitutions. 3. **Multi-heal:** approved as a separate usability change using the existing legal heal action. Start at one heal; let the player select a count and see the actual affordable cost and capped healing before applying it. A “to full” choice must be explicit and bounded by magic. Never spend beyond the selection or silently repeat after a second tap. Keep each heal's cost, hooks and report entry unchanged. Preserve actor ownership and authoritative command order on shared-phone/online paths; do not bypass table commands with a client loop. Check full/near-full health, insufficient magic, repeated taps and equivalence to the same manual heals. This checked usability change may ship under standing authorization independently of the balance experiment.
4. **Other packs:** yes. Check Goldbelly, Blazemorrow and every currently fleeable boss before a balance release. Include pending nemeses in old saves and ordinary, elite and substituted encounters. This audit need not block building the isolated prototype.
5. **Six-player scope:** the same boss-tier limit applies. Do not change `heroStrength`, `storyScale` or global `PARTY` values in this pass. For B, the whole starting encounter has at most one finite companion, including automatically added party foes; otherwise the party additions would undo the composition test. C has none. 6. **First preview:** finite composition with the corrected boss health, before exposed-round bonuses or breath changes. Measure unchanged, health-only A, B and C separately on matched scenarios/seeds so the effects are distinguishable. After correcting the party evidence below, propose one narrowly scoped breath experiment for a separate decision. Do not apply a global breath nerf or a 50%/double-damage telegraph bonus now. Inhale and Terrify are different actions; a non-damaging action is not automatically a harmless or exposed turn.
7. **Timing:** approve observation-only round and fight durations in reports. Label elapsed time honestly, including whether background time is included; optionally record foreground duration separately, without calling it thinking or decision time. Use monotonic timing during the session, tolerate missing values and old reports, and mark interrupted/reloaded intervals unavailable rather than inventing durations. Online measurements describe this device's observed time, not every player's turn time. Keep data local with existing reports, maintain the hard size bound, and do not change RNG, combat scheduling or cloud writes. This checked diagnostic addition may ship separately.

## Evidence corrections required before balance conclusions
- **The party table's “Solo” is a one-fighter party simulation.** `t19-party.mjs` calls `newPartyBattle` and `resolveParty` even for one hero. Relabel that row and add a genuine `newBattle`/`resolve` solo reference. Differences cannot be attributed only to seeds or hero samples. The current 23%-to-55% comparison is not established as actual solo versus six-player behavior.
- **“Safe” and “outcome secure” overstate the phase metric.** `t19-phase.mjs` calculates `taken = max(0, hp0 - hp1)`, a net health drop after healing, not damage taken. Use captured engine damage/healing separately. A retrospective high-health suffix among winners does not prove that defensive choices were unnecessary or that defeat was impossible. Rename it accordingly, document the exact threshold (the code checks pre- and post-resolution health), and separate win/loss duration distributions. Withdraw the “40% after the outcome is secure” conclusion pending this correction.
- **Equal progression is not equal strength.** Strengths 56–73 describe same-stage mixed-class heroes. Truly equal strength gives m=6 for six heroes and, with hpPer=1.2, an HP factor of 7. Label the sampled groups accurately. Keep individual damage/max-health ratios for mixed parties instead of treating a ratio of group means as every novice's exposure.
- **Show sample uncertainty honestly.** Ten fights per cell do not establish a general ±15-point accuracy. Report denominators, timeouts and independent campaign/seed counts, and appropriate uncertainty across independent campaigns. Do not pool two different six-player compositions into a universal zero-win claim. Preserve the known bot gaps, including revives and teamwork.
- **Keep examples and averages distinct.** The party table takes starting enemy HP from the first fight but averages boss maximum HP and company factor. Label that example or put all columns on a consistent basis. Retain reproducible source revision, seed and hero fixtures; temporary-cache-only inputs are insufficient for another agent to reproduce the result.
- **Distinguish reconstruction from recorded history.** The trace demonstrates a code path matching the report's enemy maxima and bonus. The report lacks the first five rounds and original save, so the step-5 flight, wound fraction and 651 starting boss HP are reconstructed assumptions unless separate captured evidence establishes them. 1,002 is captured maximum HP, not proof it began full. Keep this uncertainty in the diagnosis.
