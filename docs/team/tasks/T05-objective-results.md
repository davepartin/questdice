# T05 follow-up results: the Objective and Secret Objective fronts (Claude, 25 October 2026)

Build **20261025t05a**. 425 tests pass. **Presentation only.** No price, payout, wear, rule or save changed, and no WAGER / CHALLENGE / Payout wording was adopted.

**What shipped.** Grok's rev 4 fronts drawn by the shared renderer (`objectiveCardHtml` in `js/g3/cards.js`) from the game's own data, so solo and online show the same card. Two decks only: a solo Bonus Goal is an **Objective** (parchment and brass); a secret card and a private offered bribe are **Secret Objectives** (dark leather and crimson). Every card has a centre emblem that is the game's own symbol for what the goal is about (all 44 types mapped, `OBJ_FOCUS` and `OBJ_TINT`; a test fails if a card has no emblem file) and a soft tint in that colour. The chip names the deck. The terms rows are today's wording (`Stake`, `Reward`, `If not`; `Reward`, `Won, not done`, `Team defeated`, `Decline` for an offered bribe). Accept, Bet and Decline stay as buttons under the card. The painted T12 backs are untouched and stay as they are; no screen shows a card back today, so none was added (secret privacy and reveal timing are exactly as before).

**Checked.** (1) All 44 card types, staked and free, at the narrowest card a phone gets (250 px) and the widest (330 px), by `node dev/t05objective.mjs`: no text spills the frame, every image loads, the emblem keeps at least 15% of the card height (10% on the densest offered-bribe cards); contact sheets in [T05/objectives](T05/objectives). (2) The real two-phone online path (`SIZE=375x548 node dev/t05online.mjs`, also 390x664): both phones show the card from the table's real deal; an offered bribe with the longest rule ("Someone Has to Fall") and the Slacker were set on the stored table; each card is whole, the answer buttons are on screen without scrolling at 390x664, at 375x548 the densest offered card scrolls clear above the answer bar, and both phones answered through the real command path and the table moved on; no page errors. Screens: [T05/online](T05/online). (3) The solo route (Bonus Goal, poor-hero free try, secret, offered bribe) at both sizes: [T05/solo](T05/solo).

**Choices to know about.** The title is Cormorant Garamond, the game's title font, not Cinzel (the font review dropped Cinzel). On a short phone the card is sized to leave room for the answer bar: 258 px wide at 664 px tall. The smallest type is the small-caps row labels (8 to 8.5 px); values are 10.5 px or more. The sandbox cannot reach Google Fonts, so it drew with fallback fonts: **real Cormorant and Inter on an iPhone will measure a little differently, and nothing here was seen in Safari.** The old `.qc` objective CSS is left in place, unused, and can be deleted once Dave has seen the new cards live.

**Still open (not done here).** The weapon card rev 14 (3D die images, ornate frame) is a larger port and is not part of this update; the full weapon card still opens from every online row as in T21. The chip says Objective while a few game strings still say "Bonus Challenge" or "Bonus Goal"; that is a naming ruling for Astra.

## Weapon card, rev 14 in current-rule mode (build 20261025t05b)

**What shipped.** Grok's T17 rev 14 weapon card replaces the old weapon card everywhere the full card appears: the spoils thumbnails, the large card from online and solo pack rows, equipped hands and the store shelf, and the class introduction. Painted art on top (the three wide dagger paintings for daggers, the painted masters on the forge backdrop for everything else), the title, then **every face of the weapon's real die drawn as a photographed three-dimensional die** (flat red for attack, blue for block, the number and bonus symbols fitted inside each face), and metal-rimmed corner badges for the bonus types it carries. Arrows keep their ammunition badge and their arrowhead faces carry an arrow mark in the ammunition's colour. It is `weaponCardHtml` in `js/g3/cards.js`, `js/g3/dice3d.js` and `css/cards17.css`, all fed by `weaponData`, so the faces, size, sell or store price and wear label are the game's own.

**Current rules, not the proposals.** No upgrade covers, no size rows, no proposed face tables (Bronze keeps the released faces), no on-card sell button (selling and equipping stay on the screens that own them; the gold figure is a label reading Sell or Store). **Wear:** the card prints the released wear label ("6 battles left") because `wearLabel` says the game has wear, and it disappears by itself if the game ever stops wearing weapons. Crockpot's pack-mockup note says Dave decided on 25 October that weapons no longer wear, but the Director's rulings keep no-wear off until it is approved as a rule, so I did not hide a rule that is still live. Astra: please confirm which it is; either way is a one-line change.

**How the dice are drawn.** On demand, in the browser, one weapon at a time with a breath between (WebGL, three.js already in the game). Until a card's dice arrive it shows plain red or blue number tiles, and it keeps them if graphics are unavailable. Each weapon's dice are cached by their faces, and the graphics memory is given back four seconds after the last card. Measured here on software graphics (slow): 0.2 to 0.4 s of work per weapon, 14 different weapons drawn in about 11 s with the page usable throughout, and instant on a second showing. **A real iPhone has hardware graphics and was not tested: the cost there is unmeasured.**

**Checked.** All metals and sizes, shields (blue faces), Bow, Arrows with each ammunition, a legendary, at the full-card and thumbnail size, by `node dev/t05weapons.mjs`: every die photograph arrived, dice stay inside the tray, no title clipped ([sheet](T05/weapons/sheet-full.png), [thumbnails](T05/weapons/sheet-thumb.png)). The real two-phone online path: pack rows, store rows and the spoils picker open the card at 375x548 and 390x664 with Close on screen (`dev/t21cards.mjs`, `dev/t21online.mjs`); the solo route at both sizes ([T05/weapons](T05/weapons)). Hero byte-identical after looking. 427 tests pass. **Chromium with software graphics and fallback fonts, a stand-in for Firebase, not Safari.**

**Not done.** The Camp Gear pack as a 2x2 grid of card thumbnails (Crockpot's pack mockup) is waiting on Dave's view of the mockup and is not built; the pack rows stay compact, each opening this card. Diamond art exists only for the dagger and the Diamond weapon uses the Gold painting with its own badge metal, as the handoff says.
