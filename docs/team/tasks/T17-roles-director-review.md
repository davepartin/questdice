## Corrections and evidence limits

- **Distance claim:** 0.07 for Bow/Dagger is smaller than today's minimum 0.09. Remove the sentence saying no candidate pair is closer than today's closest pair. This descriptive distance is an arbitrary weighted feature summary, not a gameplay uniqueness test. Keep eight types provisionally; demonstrate useful situational choices rather than trying to maximize that scalar.
- **Uncertainty:** 40 fights do not justify a blanket “plus or minus 8 points” confidence margin or “only gaps of 20 points mean anything.” Near a 50% win rate, 8 points is roughly one standard error, not a 95% margin. A matched comparison needs the paired per-seed outcomes to estimate uncertainty. The runner currently writes aggregate rows, so the report cannot establish that a five-point change is significant, that a ten-point change is noise, or that a weapon has ceased to be an outlier. Label existing tables descriptive. Preserve raw per-seed outcomes for new runs and report paired differences/intervals, with campaign-level resampling for campaign runs. Do not rerun every old batch merely to polish a claim; narrow the wording now and add raw output to the staged runs.
- **Role copy:** the Tower Shield implementation gives **magic only**, not “magic and heal” as the role table says. The Silver Dagger currently gives pierce only; its healing comes with Gold. State metal qualifications explicitly. Special Bronze arrows still have ammunition arrowheads, so “Bronze plain/no effects” needs that exception.
- **Spear identity:** blue-face pierce is implemented: evaluate adds the effect independently of lane colour, and resolve adds pierce after normal guard subtraction. This supports the counterattack description. It does not prove the Spear is the best anti-armour purchase: the Axe has greater average attack and, at d10 Gold, 1.2 average pierce versus the Spear's 1.0, with a different block profile. Show armoured versus unarmoured matched loadouts and a targeted blue-face resolution fixture before claiming an anti-armour advantage.
- **Upgrade value:** exact prefix preservation does not guarantee every secondary output improves. For example, adding Bow faces 7 and 8 lowers average printed attack from about 1.83 to 1.38 while raising block. Record each step's tradeoff; retaining all faces must not be presented as improving every output.
- **Preview check:** `dev/weaponroles.mjs` calls `E.trainWeapon` directly, prints a prefix boolean, and takes screenshots at a hard-coded temporary path. It does not assert a successful UI purchase, check card/engine equality, reload a saved hero, fail on page errors, or exercise the online command route. Its evidence is narrower than a verified cover-purchase flow. Reuse/extend `dev/weapon-upgrades.mjs` for the preview URL, with configurable browser/output paths and explicit failing assertions.
