# T08: the Bard's targeted song, proposal and test (23 October 2026)

**Board owner:** Director (specification). **This file:** the implementation lead's proposal, asked for by Dave ("Propose exact effects and costs, then test"). The test is behind a switch that is off in the game; nothing changes for players until Director and Dave review the evidence.

## Why

The Bard's support today is real but thin (docs/balance/exp_bard/BARD_SUPPORT_2026-10-23.md, 10 matched campaigns). Lucky Verse sung for a friend gives about 4.6 healing a fight, and taking it away costs the company 1.8 points of health. Otherwise the Bard is a damage dealer that blocks the least and protects the least. Dave approved a stronger targeted-support direction: choose a song and its recipient; self-target in solo.

## Proposal: the song lives in the Bard's every-round slot (B), in place of Lucky Verse

Each round, for its magic cost, the Bard chooses **a song** and **a hero**: itself, or any standing friend.
- **Courage:** + attack for that hero this round.
- **Shelter:** + block for that hero this round. In a company it soaks that hero's own share of the blows; the friend taking the double share is the natural recipient.

Three versions to test, all at-will (once a round), scaling with the power's level like every power (`scaleNum`):

| Version | Cost | Courage | Shelter | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Steady Song** | 1 magic | +4 attack | +5 block | Same price and size as Shield Up / Shield Bash, but it can go to anyone |
| **B. Lucky Song** | 1 magic | d6: 1–2 the Bard gets 1 magic; 3–4 +4; 5–6 +7 and the Bard gets 1 magic | same roll, +1 (5 / 8) | Keeps the Bard's luck; one step stronger than today's 3 / 6 |
| **C. Big Song** | 2 magic | +8 attack | +9 block | Fewer, bigger songs; tests whether size beats frequency |

**Today's Lucky Verse** (the baseline) gives 3 or 6 attack to the Bard, or healing to a friend.

**Rule text, one sentence each (draft):**
- Steady Song: "Every round: give +4 attack (Courage) or +5 block (Shelter) to any hero, yourself included."
- Lucky Song: "Every round, roll a d6 for any hero: 1–2 you gain 1 magic; 3–4 +4 attack or +5 block; 5–6 +7 attack or +8 block, and you gain 1 magic."
- Big Song: "Every round, 2 magic: +8 attack or +9 block for any hero, yourself included."

**Magic screen (after the test, if adopted):**
- the song's two choices (Courage, Shelter) as two buttons;
- below them, the recipients as small hero portraits (the painted miniature head), each with name and health ("Ann 18/34"), the one taking the double share marked;
- in solo, only the Bard's own portrait, selected.

This reuses the existing "For me / For a friend" picker (`.pd-for` in `js/g3/powers.js`).

## How it is tested

- **Engine:** `CLASS2.song = 'steady' | 'lucky' | 'big'` (off in the game). `castPower(b, 'luckyverse', { to, song: 'atk' | 'block' })`. A song for a friend travels like today's verse and lands on the friend's attack or block before the blows.
- **Simulated Bard:**
  - Shelter on the most hurt friend below 60% health, or on the hero likely to take the double share when a big hit is coming;
  - Shelter on itself when it is below half;
  - otherwise Courage on itself.
  - In solo, Shelter when the coming hit is large against its health, otherwise Courage.
- **Matched comparison:** the same 10 four-hero campaigns with Bards as the base Bard run (docs/balance/exp_bard), arms base / A / B / C. Measured: team battles won, falls, health left, rounds, the support given per fight, and the Bards' own solo results (each hero also plays the campaign alone).
- **Success:**
  - friends' falls and lowest health improve clearly beyond today's verse (more than about 2 points of health a fight);
  - the Bard's solo play does not jump far past the other classes;
  - battle length does not grow.
