# T17 cumulative size ladder: measured tables (a proposal, not a rule)

Generated by `node tools/t17-faces.mjs` (ladders: `tools/t17-ladder.mjs`, JSON: `T17-faces.json`). Nothing in the game uses these faces. **This replaces the earlier table, which preserved only the d4 faces and is withdrawn.**

**The ladder.** One ladder for every weapon, metal and ammunition variant: d4 faces are fixed; d6 appends two faces; d8 appends two more; d10 appends two more. A face's full identity (colour, number, bonus plus, every effect, ammunition marking) never changes once it is on the ladder. The appended faces are the two best faces of what today's released spread at that size has beyond the faces already on the ladder (highest hit, then most icons). **Checks, run on every row below and in `test/t17faces.test.mjs`:** each size has exactly its die size in faces, and d4, d6 and d8 are exact prefixes of the next size. Covered: all eight active types (Dagger, Sword, Axe, Spear, Round Shield, Tower Shield, Bow, Arrows) × Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Arrows in all four ammunitions (Normal, Piercing, Fire, Frost).

**Reading the columns.** Each face is equally likely. E[attack] = the sum of the hit (number plus bonus plus) of the attack faces, divided by the number of faces; E[block] likewise for block faces. Effect columns give how often each effect is on the die (share of faces) and how many icons per roll. Where a purchase LOWERS an effect's share of faces, the row is marked **dilutes** even if the number of icons rose.

**Summary over all 99 purchases (every weapon, metal and ammunition):** 18 dilute at least one effect's share of faces; 28 lower E[attack] or E[block]. The per-row detail is below; the combined "average hit" used in the earlier table is not used here.

## Diamond (proposal for review, not designed in the game)

**Package.** The weapon's Gold ladder (same colours, numbers, identity and arrowhead markings, so shields stay block weapons) plus a crit step: the best face of the d4 set, and the best face appended at each size, carries one more of its icon (or gets one if it had none). The extra icons never move, so the Diamond ladder is cumulative too (checked). A permanent tier, same d4 to d10 sizes bought at the same prices, no automatic maximum size. This is deliberately modest: a Diamond is a Gold weapon with a growing crit, not a different weapon.

**Source, for the Director to rule on (nothing implemented):** a rare drop from the **final Act III boss only** (Vharaxis, the Ember Queen, the step 10 boss), 30% on a first victory over it, at most one Diamond per hero, never in the shop, starting at **d6** (two covers already open, like a found d6). Eligible types: Dagger, Sword, Axe, Spear, Round Shield, Tower Shield (the one-handed active types; Bow and Arrows are paired items and are not offered as Diamonds in the first experiment).
