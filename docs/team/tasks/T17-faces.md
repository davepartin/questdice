# T17 nested-faces tables (measured, not a rule)

Generated by `node tools/t17-faces.mjs` from the released data (`applyRelease()` then `weaponFaces`). Nothing in the game changed.

**Rule measured (proposal for review):** a weapon's d4 faces are fixed; a bigger size keeps them and adds (size − 4) faces, the highest-hit faces of today's spread at that size. "Avg hit" = mean of face number plus attack/block bonus. "Useful" = share of faces that hit for 3 or more or carry a bonus icon. "Bonus icons" = total bonus icons on the faces. "Today" = the released spread at that size; "Nested" = the proposal.

Reading: a nested die is never worse at its top end, but it gains faces slowly, so the average of the extra faces is what matters; the Delta column is Nested minus Today.
