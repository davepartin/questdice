# T22 attack feedback: results (Claude, 25 October 2026)

Brief: [T22-attack-feedback-and-coordination.md](T22-attack-feedback-and-coordination.md). Evidence in [T22/](T22). **Engine results are unchanged** (this is display only; the table, report and random numbers are exactly what they were).

**Cause, found in the source and reproduced.** When a company round replays, every phone already holds the round's FINAL table. After each hero's blow the replay repainted the monster's plate from that final table, so the first hero's blow dropped the plate to the health left after everybody's blows, and every later blow showed nothing: Dave's observation exactly. Reproduced on two real browsers over the online path (stand-in for Firebase, not Safari) with the old painting restored: Dave1 strikes for 13 (7 blocked), the plate immediately shows 363, the health after both heroes' blows, although the other hero has not yet struck; JP's 24 then changes nothing. The picture is [attack-before-2heroes-after-first-strike.jpg](T22/attack-before-2heroes-after-first-strike.jpg): the cue says "Duck −7" and "−13" while the plate already reads 363 (it started at 400). The engine's rule was never at fault: block is a shared pool per monster spent in order, so the first blow here consumed 7 and the second took no block.

**The change.** A replayed company round now keeps a staged health: each monster starts the replay at its health before the round, and each strike takes off only its own real loss (and its splash on the others), in the engine's report order; each hero's bar starts at their health before the blows and steps down by each monster act's real share. The plates and bars are repainted from the real table once the round is over, so a catch-up never leaves a wrong number. Other repaints during the replay no longer jump the staged numbers. Files: `js/g3/battle3d.js`, `js/g3/flat.js` (the plate painter takes a staged health). The blocked cue (`Duck −7`), the damage number and the pierce mark were already per strike and are untouched. A fallen hero never strikes in the report, so no hit is invented for one.

**The ordered UI event trace.** The battle controller records `plate`, `strike` and `hero` events in order (`B3.traceStart()` and `B3.traceRead()`); the online check reads it on every phone and compares it to the report. `node dev/t22attack.mjs [heroes] [label]`.

**Checked** (every number is the real report of a real round, on every phone at the table):
- Two heroes, two phones: plate 400 → 363 → 334 on both phones, each strike named with its own loss; after the round the plate shows the table's health, 334. Baseline run: 363 after the first strike on both phones (the defect).
- Four heroes, four phones, one monster: 135 → 108 → 78 → 51 → 30 on all four phones in the same order (Pal4 27, Dave1 30, Pal2 27, Pal3 21); the table ends at 30. [Picture after the first strike](T22/attack-after-4heroes-after-first-strike.jpg).
- A kill before a later hero's turn (monster set to 45): only two strikes appear, 35 then the killing 10 (plate 45 → 10 → 0); the other two heroes do not strike and no hit is shown for them. [Picture](T22/attack-kill-4heroes-after-first-strike.jpg).
- A stalled and resumed view (the second phone's live feed stopped for the whole round): it plays the same two strikes once, same order, ending at the table's health; nothing is replayed twice.
- A fully blocked blow: seen in the baseline round (7 of the first blow blocked, shown as a block cue and the reduced loss); no separate all-blocked fixture was built.

**Not verified.** Reduced motion (the staged numbers do not depend on the animation, but I did not capture a reduced-motion run), mixed pierce with splash and Ward retaliation on a real table (the staging follows the report's splash lists but I built no fixture for them), a live recording (the trace stands in for it: it is ordered and the same on every phone, but it is not a video and shows no timing), real Safari, real Firestore, real fonts. No balance change; no multi-heal or timing change made here.
