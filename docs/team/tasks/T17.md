# T17 — Weapon card plan: one card per metal tier, size uncovering, no wear

**Status:** APPROVED for isolated gameplay experiment (preview only), NOT live release; 40/80/140 upgrade prices provisional. Docs only so far; nothing in the game has changed.
**Owners:** Grok Bot for the revised mockup; Claude for gameplay implementation and economy checks.
**Source:** Dave's decisions, 24 Oct 2026.

## Dave's decisions
1. **Weapons never wear out.** Remove battles-left / durability entirely: one less thing to track. This replaces the earlier ideas that the Bow never decays but Arrows do, and that Diamond lasts 1 battle.
2. **One card per metal tier per weapon** (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond). A Silver Dagger is the same card for all four sizes, d4 through d10.
3. **Bigger sizes start covered** by a "cardboard" piece the player taps and buys to remove, which raises the weapon's size.
4. **Call it weapon SIZE** (= die size), never "strength", on the card. Open question: how size relates to the existing hero strength / `heroStrength` cap. Do **not** change `heroStrength`.
5. **Top-right corner of the card:** a square matching the battle-map dice-grid square for that weapon, with the current-size die drawn sitting on it — as if you took the die off the battle grid after the round and set it on the card. When a size is bought, the die on the card grows (e.g. a d6 Dagger shows a d6; the d8 and d10 spaces stay covered until bought).
6. **Nested faces stay as proposed** (T16): faces 1–4 fixed, a bigger die only adds faces, and the metal sets the bonus icon count.
7. **Corner badges** show only the bonus types the faces add (attack, gold, health, pierce, magic), as already built in PR #46.

## Ruling (Astra, Oct 24, 2026)
1. WEAR: Remove wear from all weapons, including Bow, Arrows and Diamond. No breakage, repairs, restocking charges or battles-left counter.
2. CARD IDENTITY: One card design per weapon and metal: Bronze, Silver, Gold and Diamond. Each owned weapon keeps its own purchased size. Buying another Silver Dagger does not inherit the first dagger's upgrades. Metal and size are separate. Size upgrades do not change metal.
3. SIZE UPGRADE COSTS: Start with these test prices: d4→d6 40 gold, d6→d8 80 gold, d8→d10 140 gold. Use the same prices across weapon types and metals initially. Pay one step at a time. A found d8 already has its smaller sizes uncovered. These prices need economy testing before release.
4. HERO STRENGTH: For this experiment, weapon SIZE determines the weapon die. Do not add a strength requirement or change heroStrength. Keep hand/body dice and their existing effects separate. Do not charge twice through both the old weapon-slot upgrade and the new card upgrade. Claude must identify and replace any overlapping upgrade path in the preview. An unopened equipment slot remains unopened. A bigger weapon does not unlock another hand.
5. GOLD WITHOUT WEAR: The main purchases are weapon sizes, better metals, alternative equipment, existing hero upgrades and supplies. Do not add another maintenance system to replace wear. Test whether these purchases are enough first. Buying size upgrades must not increase sell value in this first experiment. Keep sale proceeds below purchase cost and check for buy/sell exploits. Make the lack of an upgrade refund clear before payment.
6. DIAMOND: Diamond is permanent, rare endgame equipment. Its distinction is its bonus-face package and presentation, not a temporary lifespan. It uses the same d4–d10 sizes and does not automatically give maximum size. Keep it out of the ordinary early-game shop. Bring the proposed Diamond face tables for review before implementing their balance.
7. CAPACITY: Keep four pack slots plus two equipped hands, six weapon cards total. Bow and Arrows each occupy one equipped hand and one card. Diamond uses a normal slot. Do not redesign legacy two-handed equipment in this task.
8. NESTED FACES: Approved: increasing size preserves all earlier faces and adds the new faces. Show which faces are currently available and which remain locked. Do not assume a bigger die is always better. Compare average output and useful-face frequency for each upgrade.
9. CARD PRESENTATION: Use SIZE terminology, covered upgrade slots and a three-dimensional die resting on the matching equipment-slot square. The square must reflect the actual equipped position. An unequipped card should not falsely imply that it occupies a hand. Corner badges summarize bonus types actually present on currently unlocked faces. Include block if a weapon has a block bonus. Badges are a legend, not additional bonuses.
10. DELIVERY: Grok owns the revised mockup. Claude owns gameplay implementation and economy checks. Keep this separate from the presentation-only integration and preserve live saves. Before release, provide an old-save migration proposal. Do not reset heroes or silently convert their equipment.

## Director review of plan and rev 2, 24 October 2026

**Grok:** the standard-color d6 equipped rev 2 screenshot is accepted as the visual direction for the isolated experiment. The restored frame, explicit ATK/BLK marks, three visible die faces and equipment wording address the requested corrections. This is a static screenshot review only; the dark-stone alternative, all other sizes and purchase interactions have not been independently checked. Keep the standard-color version as the integration target. No more speculative paintings or redesigns are needed now. Coordinate directly with Claude on the board when corrected face data is ready; update numerical mockups from that data rather than treating the current numbers as approved.

**Claude: a blocking issue in the face proposal, not in released gameplay.** In `tools/t17-faces.mjs`, `nested()` starts from d4 each time and selects the strongest `size - 4` faces from that size's released spread. This protects only the original four faces. It does not preserve the faces bought at d6 when buying d8, or those bought at d8 when buying d10. The ladder then slices the last two faces without first checking that the earlier prefix is identical. Consequently the table is not evidence for the approved cumulative rule.

Next action:
1. Build one cumulative ladder per weapon, metal and ammunition variant: d4, append two faces for d6, append two for d8, append two for d10. Preserve full face identity, including attack/block, value, plus, effects and ammunition markings.
2. Add exact prefix-equality checks for all three transitions and assert the number of faces equals the die size. Cover all eight active weapon types and supported ammunition variants. Correct the plan's reference to six types.
3. Regenerate the measurements and mockup data. Separate attack and block averages and effect frequencies; the present combined hit/useful metric is insufficient to establish that every upgrade is better. Adding faces can dilute an existing effect even when average numerical output increases. Do not characterize the entire change as a small buff based only on Silver Dagger.
4. Return corrected tables and proposed Diamond faces for review. Namespace isolation, switch-off baseline and purchase infrastructure may proceed independently under the existing preview-only authorization. Do not publish unreviewed face balance as settled or activate it at the live root.

Answers to the four plan questions:
- **Faces:** paid size upgrades should offer worthwhile progression. Do not deliberately add blanks just to force a disadvantage. Top-end additions are an acceptable candidate, but only with genuinely cumulative faces and corrected measurements; the current table is not approved.
- **Hand upgrade prices:** leave them unchanged for the first comparison. Measure their remaining value without the weapon ceiling before changing another price system.
- **Diamond source:** use a rare late-campaign boss reward as the proposed source, not the ordinary shop. Submit the exact eligible encounter, chance, starting size and face package together for review; do not invent these silently. Diamond acquisition remains held while the rest of the isolated experiment proceeds. The current special-case Diamond table is not the proposed permanent tier: it even shows six faces for d4. Proposed tables must have the correct count, preserve weapon identity and cover shields, Bow and ammunition as well as attack weapons.
- **Sale value:** approve half the as-found worth for the initial experiment, with no wear discount and no refund for purchased size increases. Keep the as-found basis stable across save/load and transfers, and verify buy/upgrade/sell/rebuy cannot profit. The migration statement “so selling never pays a windfall” is not established: removing wear discounts may increase an old worn weapon's sale value. Quantify that difference in the migration proposal; live-save migration is not approved by this note.

T05 PR #49 remains a separate pending integration review. This T17 review does not approve merging T05 or releasing T17. No new phone testing or message forwarding is required from Dave.
