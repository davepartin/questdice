# Wizard magic cards (t422, mockup only)

Numbers come from `js/data.js` / `js/engine.js` unless marked **PROPOSED**. Upgrades cost 50 gold (Level II) and 90 gold (Level III).
Dice are the colour of what they add (Arc Bolt's d8s are red, for attack), with white numbers.
Identity: big elemental magic that hits more than one thing, and turns the fight sideways.

## Final Wizard set
| | Power | One line | Cost | Level I / II / III | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Start** | Arc Bolt | Buy red d8 attack dice; the bolt arcs, so **half the total hits every other monster** (PROPOSED), up to 6 dice (PROPOSED). | 2 per die | 1d8 / 2d8 / 3d8 for 2 magic, +1d8 per 2 more | The dice toy, with a lightning twist: one roll, the whole room lights up. |
| **Start** | Polymorph (**PROPOSED**) | Turn the target into a frog: it can't attack this round. | 2, once a battle | frog / frog + 4 pierce on it / frog for 1 magic | The funniest, most creative trick. It makes kids laugh and teaches reading the monster's big hit. |
| **Start** | Storm Coil | Store 1 magic a round, then release attack; half splashes on the others. | 1 per charge | 4 charges: 24 / 32 / 40 (PROPOSED per-charge 5 → 4) | The store-and-release crescendo: a stored storm going off. |
| Unlock | Meteor (live) | Round 3 or later: attack = 4 × the round number; half splashes. | 5 | 4/8/12/16 by round; +25% / +50% by level | The aspirational late-fight nuke; it rewards long fights. |
| Unlock | Healing Light (live) | Store, then release healing. | 1 per charge | 4 charges: heal 32 / 40 / 48 | Support for parties, once players know the loop. |
| Unlock | Magic Missile (live) | +2 pierce every round. | 1 a round | +2 / +3 / +4 | Steady and simple, a good pick for armoured monsters. |

**Cut:** Barrier (+4 block a round) is the same as the Knight's Shield Up. Wizards turn danger sideways (Polymorph) instead of tanking it.

## Balance (value per magic at Level I, 2 monsters; same method as KNIGHT.md)
| Card | Value per magic | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Arc Bolt (PROPOSED arcs) | 4.5 attack + 2.25 splash per 2 magic ≈ **3.4**, ≈ 4.5 at 3 monsters | The live version (no arcs) is 2.25: too low next to Aimed Shot's 4.5. The arcs fix it, on theme. |
| Polymorph | stops one blow (~6-8 damage × 0.8) per 2 magic ≈ **3** | Better against big hitters, so timing matters. |
| Storm Coil (PROPOSED per 4) | (24 + 12 splash) / 4 ≈ **9** at 4 charges (live per 5: 10.5, too hot) | Matches Rally (8.2) and Volley (7.8). |
| Meteor | round 4: 16 + 8 splash / 5 ≈ 4.8 | Fine for an unlock. |
| Healing Light | 32 / 4 = 8 | Fine. |
| Magic Missile | 2.6 | Fine; it's cheap. |
Starters across heroes, roughly: Ranger 4.5 / 3.5 / 7.8, Knight 5.3 / 4.5 / 8.2, Wizard 3.4-4.5 / 3 / 9. Close enough. The Wizard does best against crowds, the Ranger against one tough target.
