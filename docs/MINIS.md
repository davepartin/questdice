# QuestDice miniature catalog

Machine-readable source: `minis/catalog.json`. Rebuild this page with `node tools/minis-md.mjs`. Do not edit this file by hand.

## For campaign writers (and QuestStoryBot)

Cast every fight from this catalog **by `id`**. You may rename a mini, revoice it, and give it a new job in the story. You may not invent a picture or point a fight at art that is not listed here.

- Pick the miniature whose **title** and **description** match the creature you need. `reskinAs` is a hint list, not a limit.
- Put that `id` in the pack's `foes` / `fights` list. Set a display name beside it if the story name differs from the catalog name.
- If no miniature fits, **request a new painted mini** rather than stretching a wrong one or describing unseen art.
- Comic-tone minis (`puffball`, `moonjelly`, `yeti`, and the already-funny Heist pieces) are rare relief. At most one written comic step per act; never a finale.
- Merged packs keep their locked casts. New campaigns should still choose from these ids.

**50 monsters** and **15 hero looks**. Cast by `id`. Rename freely. Do not invent art.
