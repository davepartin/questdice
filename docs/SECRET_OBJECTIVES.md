# Secret Objectives (design, Oct 2026)

Dave: "There are a lot of games that have this sort of secret objective that are helpful, sometimes hurtful to the team, but
that you don't know what's happening is the fun of it." Bribes are not a separate system: a bribe is a secret objective with one
extra rule (you may **Accept** or **Decline** it).

## The rhythm of a campaign step

1. **Camp**: gear, the Camp Store, upgrades.
2. **The road**: an encounter (a choice or a dice test) or the story's page.
3. **The Envelope**: every hero gets a secret objective on their own phone.
4. **The battle.**
5. **Rewards**: the step's spoils, then the envelopes are opened (the Reveal) and objectives pay out.
6. Back to **camp**.

Every battle has envelopes, at every table size from 2 to 6 heroes, and solo too (a solo hero gets challenge cards only).

## The Envelope screen

- Every phone shows a short paragraph at the same moment. The writer's pack can give it a voice (the Magpie King whispers in The
  Clover Heist; a cheese-loving ghost in Rival Duo); the default voice is **Old Whisper**, a ghost who runs a pawn shop and cannot
  keep his voice down.
- Every hero must tap to continue, and every phone has **two buttons in the same places** so nobody can tell who had a choice:
  ordinary cards show **Got it** and **Hmm…**; a bribe shows **Accept** and **Decline**.
- The table rule, shown on the screen: **you may not say or show what your card says.** You may say anything else, including lies.
- "Lead the Charge" is common, so it is the card everyone claims to have.

## How cards are dealt

| Heroes | Cover cards | Rivalries | Challenges | Bribes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (solo) | — | — | always | — |
| 2 | often | one pair now and then (both heroes get the same goal, only one wins) | often | rare (1 in 8 battles) |
| 3–4 | about a third | one pair most battles | about a third | about 1 battle in 4 |
| 5–6 | about a third | one or two pairs, or a three-way race | about a third | about 1 battle in 3 (never two at once) |

- A rivalry is always dealt to **two or more heroes**, and the card never hints that anyone else has it.
- No hero gets a bribe two battles running.
- A boss battle deals **Final Envelopes**: the same kinds of card with bigger rewards.

## Paying out

- **Cover cards, rivalries and challenges pay only if the team wins.** Selfishness has to stay small enough to still win.
- A rivalry is won by the hero who did it best. A tie splits the prize.
- Rewards grow with the act. **G** below means 40 gold in Act I, 70 in Act II, 100 in Act III.
- Bribes have their own table (see below).

## The Reckoning (the vote)

With 2 or more heroes, after **every** battle each hero answers one quick question: **"Did anyone take a bribe?"** They can name a
hero or tap **Nobody**. The vote happens every time, so holding one gives nothing away.

- **Everyone else names the hero who took a bribe:** they are caught. The bribe is lost, and the accusers share it as **Honor**.
- **The vote names a hero who took no bribe:** that hero gets a small **Wrongly Accused** bonus (G/4).
- **No bribe was dealt and everyone said Nobody:** a small **Trust** bonus for all (G/4 each).

Then **the Reveal**: every phone shows every hero's card and whether they did it. Rivalries get a drum-roll: "**WizKid and
Smalls were BOTH chasing gold!** Smalls wins, 41 to 38."

## Bribes: win or lose

| What happened | Result |
|---|---|
| Team wins, bribe met, not caught | The bribe pays |
| Team wins, bribe met, caught | The accusers share it |
| Team wins, bribe failed | Nothing extra |
| Team loses, the hero accepted | Normal defeat, plus the spirit takes a cut (lose G/2 more gold) |
| Team loses, the hero declined | **Honor**: G/2 and a Hall of Fame mark |
| Team loses and every bribe offered that battle was accepted | The spirit laughs: "a fine bunch of crooks". Everyone loses G/2 more |

## The cards

Each card is a rule the game can check (it already counts damage, blocks, healing, gold, kills, triples, straights, rounds and
who the monsters targeted). The writer's pack may rename and re-voice any card to fit its story; the rule stays the same.

### Cover cards (help the team)

| Card | The rule | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead the Charge** | Win this battle on the first try | G/2 |
| **The Shield** | Be the monsters' target in 2 or more rounds and still be standing at the end | G |
| **Field Medic** | Heal friends for 15+ health (thrown potions, revives) | G |
| **No One Left Behind** | No hero is down when the battle ends | G |
| **Quick Work** | Win in 4 rounds or fewer | G |
| **Spark Plug** | Share magic with a friend twice | G/2 + 2 magic next fight |

### Rivalries (dealt to 2 or more heroes; the best one wins)

| Card | Most of… | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| **Gold Rush** | gold earned this battle | double the gold you earned (up to 2G) |
| **Arcane Ace** | damage from magic powers | G |
| **The Finisher** | killing blows | G |
| **Stone Wall** | damage blocked | G |
| **Big Swing** | the biggest single round of damage | G |
| **Lucky Streak** | triples rolled | G |
| **Straight Shooter** | straights rolled | G |
| **Fleet Foot** | total feet rolls across the battle (the feet die) | G |
| **Strong Arm** | total hand rolls across the battle (the hand dice) | G |
| **Bright Spark** | total head rolls across the battle (the head die) | G |
| **Crowd Favourite** | battle points | G and a Hall of Fame mark |

### Challenges (yours alone; dice-themed, small and fun)

| Card | The rule | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| **Three Times Lucky** | A triple in 3 different rounds | G |
| **Matching Mittens** | Both hand dice show the same number, twice | G/2 |
| **Big Thinker** | Roll your head die's top face | G/2 |
| **Never Last** | Never be the slowest hero (team games) or always win initiative (solo) | G/2 |
| **Teetotaler** | Win without drinking a potion | G/2 |
| **Big Game Hunter** | Land the killing blow on the toughest monster | G |
| **Thrifty** | Use no magic rerolls | G/2 |
| **Heart Match** | Your heart die matches your head die in some round | G/2 |
| **Cliffhanger** | Win while at 5 health or less | G |
| **Saver** | End the battle with more magic than you started with | G/2 |
| **Talent Show** | Both talent dice pay in the same round | G/2 |

### Bribes (Accept or Decline; bad for the team, good for you)

| Card | The rule | Reward if it pays |
|---|---|---|
| **The Slacker** | Deal the least damage of anyone | 2G |
| **Glass Shield** | Block nothing in 2 rounds | 2G |
| **Cold Shoulder** | Do not help anyone (no potion, shared magic or revive) when a friend is below half health | 2G |
| **The Long Fight** | The battle lasts 5 rounds or more | 2G |
| **Spare That One** | Leave one named monster alive until round 4 | 2G |
| **The Hoarder** | End with 6+ magic unspent | 2G |
| **Golden Fumble** | Take the Gold side of every straight you roll | 2G |
| **The Pact** | You cannot be knocked out this battle, but you deal half damage | 2G |
| **The Framer** | The vote names a hero who did not take a bribe | 2G |

## Building it, step by step

1. **Envelopes and the Reveal** for cover cards, rivalries and challenges; payouts on the rewards screen. Measure the two
   numbers the game does not count yet: damage from magic powers, and the biggest single round of damage.
2. **Bribes**: Accept/Decline, the Reckoning vote, Honor, the bribe table.
3. **Voices and stats**: writer packs re-voice the cards and the spirit; the Hall of Fame tracks rivalries won, challenges
   completed, bribes taken, bribes declined, times caught, Honor; the host picks Envelopes **Every battle / Sometimes / Off**.

A note on secrets: a team game travels between phones as one shared table. Among friends that is fine; a curious player with
browser tools could peek. If the game ever goes public, the envelopes should move to a small server.
