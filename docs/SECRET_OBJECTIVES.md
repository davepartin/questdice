# Secret Objectives (design, Oct 2026)

Dave: "There are a lot of games that have this sort of secret objective that are helpful, sometimes hurtful to the team, but
that you don't know what's happening is the fun of it." And: "It's these sort of bad but not too bad outcomes that makes the game
complex, but right on the edge."

Bribes are not a separate system. A bribe is a secret objective that is bad for the team; some are **offered** (Accept or
Decline) and some are **forced** ("you have been chosen"). There is **no vote**: the truth comes out on the rewards screen, and
that is the fun.

## The rhythm of a campaign step

1. **Camp**: gear, the Camp Store, upgrades.
2. **The road**: an encounter (a choice or a dice test) or the story's page.
3. **The Envelope**: every hero gets a secret objective on their own phone.
4. **The battle.**
5. **Rewards**: the step's spoils, then **the Reveal** (every card, and what each hero did with it) and the objectives' payouts.
6. Back to **camp**.

Every battle has envelopes, at every table size from 2 to 6 heroes. A solo hero gets challenge cards only.

## The Envelope screen

Every phone shows, at the same moment:

1. **The scene** (the same for everyone, about 25 words): the voice leans in. The writer's pack gives it a voice (the Magpie King
   in The Clover Heist, a cheese-loving ghost in Rival Duo); the default is **Old Whisper**, a ghost who runs a pawn shop and
   cannot keep his voice down.
2. **Your card** (yours alone, about 30 words of flavor, then the rule and the reward in one plain line).
3. **The cover line**: "Keep it secret. If anyone asks, say: *"…"*" Every card that battle carries the **same** quote, so it
   proves nothing. It is chosen from:
   - *"Mine's about winning the fight. Let's go."*
   - *"Mine says keep everyone standing and win."*
   - *"Mine just wants us to win, and quickly."*
   - *"Mine's a team one. Win the battle."*
4. **Two buttons in the same places on every phone**, so nobody can tell from fingers or timing who had a choice:
   - most cards: **Got it** and **Hmm…** (both continue);
   - an **offered** bribe: **Accept** and **Decline**;
   - a **forced** bribe: **Got it** and **Hmm…** (it looks exactly like an ordinary card).

The whole screen takes about 15 seconds for everyone to read, so nobody finishes suspiciously fast. A Continue arrives only when
every phone has tapped.

## How cards are dealt

| Heroes | Cover cards | Rivalries | Challenges | Bribes (offered or forced) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (solo) | — | — | always | — |
| 2 | often (sometimes both heroes get Lead the Charge) | one pair now and then (both heroes, same goal, one winner) | often | about 1 battle in 6 |
| 3–4 | about a third | one pair most battles | about a third | about 1 battle in 3 |
| 5–6 | about a third | one or two pairs, or a three-way race | about a third | about 1 battle in 2 (at most one offered and one forced) |

- A rivalry is always dealt to **two or more heroes**, and the card never hints that anyone else has it.
- No hero gets a bribe two battles running.
- A boss battle deals **Final Envelopes**: the same cards with bigger rewards.

## Paying out

- **Cover cards, rivalries and challenges pay only if the team wins.** Selfishness has to stay small enough to still win.
- A rivalry is won by the hero who did it best; a tie splits the prize.
- Rewards grow with the act. **G** below means 40 gold in Act I, 70 in Act II, 100 in Act III.

### Bribes: win or lose

| What happened | Result |
|---|---|
| Offered, **accepted**, team wins, bribe met | **2G** |
| Offered, accepted, team wins, bribe failed | Nothing |
| Offered, **accepted**, team **loses** | Normal defeat, and the voice takes a cut: lose **G/2** more |
| Offered, **declined**, team **loses** | **Honor**: G/2 and a Hall of Fame mark |
| Offered, declined, team wins | A little Honor: G/4 |
| **Forced**, team wins, bribe met | **G** |
| Forced, team loses | Nothing extra (you had no choice, and nobody can be sure of that) |
| Team loses and every offered bribe that battle was accepted | The voice laughs: "a fine bunch of crooks". Everyone loses G/2 more |

## The Reveal (on the rewards screen)

Every phone shows every card, in turn, with a line each:

- "**Jared took the bribe from the Lunch King to earn the most gold, and did it.** Jared receives 100 gold."
- "**Smalls was forced to spare the goblin chief until round 4.** Smalls managed it, grumbling. 40 gold."
- "**WizKid declined the bribe.** The team lost anyway; WizKid keeps the Honor (35 gold)."
- "**WizKid and Smalls were BOTH chasing gold!** Smalls wins, 41 to 38."
- "**Ada had the honest card: Lead the Charge.** Ada really did just want to win."

(Lines use the hero's name, never "he" or "she".) The Hall of Fame keeps: rivalries won, challenges completed, bribes taken,
bribes declined, bribes forced, Honor.

## The cards

Each card is a rule the game can check: it already counts damage, blocks, healing, gold, kills, triples, straights, rounds, knock-
outs and who the monsters targeted. Two new counts are needed: damage from magic powers, and the biggest single round of damage.
The writer's pack may re-voice any card to fit its story; the rule stays the same. Every card below has its default flavor.

### Cover cards (help the team)

| Card | Flavor | The rule | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lead the Charge** | "No tricks this time. The voice just wants a good show, and a good show ends with the monsters running." | Win this battle on the first try | G/2 |
| **The Shield** | "Someone has to stand at the front. The voice thinks it should be you. It is very rarely wrong about faces." | Be the monsters' target in 2+ rounds and still be standing at the end | G |
| **Field Medic** | "You smell of herbs and good intentions. The voice wants you to use both." | Heal friends for 15+ health (thrown potions, revives) | G |
| **No One Left Behind** | "Nobody gets left on the ground today. Not one. The voice has had enough funerals this week." | No hero is down when the battle ends | G |
| **Quick Work** | "The voice is late for lunch. Make it quick." | Win in 4 rounds or fewer | G |
| **Spark Plug** | "You have more magic than sense. Share some." | Share magic with a friend twice | G/2, and 2 magic next fight |

### Bribes (bad for the team, good for you)

**Offered** (Accept or Decline). These are the brutal ones:

| Card | Flavor | The rule | Pays |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Slacker** | "Why work so hard? Let the others do it. Nobody will notice. Probably." | Deal the least damage of anyone | 2G |
| **Glass Shield** | "Lower your guard. Just for a little while. The voice wants to see what happens." | Block nothing in 2 rounds | 2G |
| **Cold Shoulder** | "They can look after themselves. You have your own problems." | Help nobody (no potion, shared magic or revive) while a friend is below half health | 2G |
| **The Long Fight** | "Make it last. The voice has popcorn." | The battle lasts 5 rounds or more | 2G |
| **The Pact** | "The monster makes you a promise: it will never knock you out today. In return, you go easy on it." | You cannot be knocked out this battle, but you deal half damage | 2G |
| **Someone Has to Fall** | "Not you. Never you. But someone on your side has to hit the ground before this is over." | Another hero is knocked out at least once (a revive still counts) | 2G |
| **Last One Standing** | "When the dust settles, the voice wants you to be the healthiest one there." | End with more health than every teammate | 2G |

**Forced** ("You have been chosen"). Milder, but no choice, and nobody can tell it from an ordinary card:

| Card | Flavor | The rule | Pays |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Golden Fumble** | "You have been chosen. Gold, gold, gold. You cannot help yourself." | Take the Gold side of every straight you roll | G |
| **Spare That One** | "You have been chosen. That one there is the voice's cousin. Be nice." | Leave one named monster alive until round 4 | G |
| **The Hoarder** | "You have been chosen. Keep your magic close. Do not spend it all." | End with 6+ magic unspent | G |
| **Lone Wolf** | "You have been chosen. Work alone. Never hit the same monster as a teammate." | Never attack the monster a teammate attacks that round | G |

## Building it, step by step

1. **Envelopes and the Reveal** for cover cards, rivalries and challenges; payouts on the rewards screen; the two new counts
   (magic damage, biggest round).
2. **Bribes**: offered (Accept/Decline) and forced, the bribe payout table, Honor, the "crooks" line.
3. **Voices and stats**: writer packs re-voice the cards and the voice; the Hall of Fame tracks it all; the host picks Envelopes
   **Every battle / Sometimes / Off**.

A note on secrets: a team game travels between phones as one shared table. Among friends that is fine; a curious player with
browser tools could peek. If the game ever goes public, the envelopes should move to a small server.
