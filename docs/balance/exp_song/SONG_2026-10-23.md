# The Bard's targeted song, tested (T08, 23 October 2026)

Proposal: [docs/team/tasks/T08.md](../../team/tasks/T08.md). Behind `CLASS2.song` (off in the game).

The arms differ only in the Bard's every-round power. ## What it says

1. **The song reaches friends far more than the verse did:** 7–10 points of attack or block a fight, against 4.6 healing. Targeted support does happen.
2. **But the table barely notices.** T08's success test was friends clearly better off than today (more than about 2 points of health a fight). No arm passes on health left. Lucky Song is the only one that moves falls beyond its error (−2.1 ± 1.2, better in 7 of 10 campaigns), and it also shortens battles a little.
3. **Steady Song lost boss fights that today's Bard wins** (95% against 100%). With 20 boss fights an arm that is not yet a pattern, but it does not argue for Steady.
4. Four-hero companies already win 97% of battles, so a support change has little room to show in wins; falls are the better measure.

## Recommendation (for the Director and Dave)

- If the targeted song is adopted, **Lucky Song (B)** is the one with evidence: fewer falls, slightly shorter fights, and the Bard keeps its luck.
- It does not make the Bard a strong supporter on its own. A bigger lift would need the song to count more in the moment it matters (Shelter on the friend taking the double share) rather than more often.
- This run's tables do not separate the Bard's own solo strength under each song; check that before any release.
- The Magic-screen design (hero portraits with names and health) waits for that decision.
