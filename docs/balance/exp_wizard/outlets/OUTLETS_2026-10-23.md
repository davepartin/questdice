# Wizard: the existing magic outlets, tested (T07, 23 October 2026)

Dave: "proceed with the existing-outlet experiment. Verify the simulated player considers Heart Change and partial-charge releases."

**Setup:**
- 5 independent campaigns, Act II Wizards, the four matchup fights, 240 fights an arm on matched seeds;
- the wise (look-ahead) player, now allowed to turn the heart die (NUDGE=1);
- changes are paired within each campaign, ± is across campaigns;
- variants.txt and the per-fight rows (*.json) are in this folder.

## The simulated player now uses those outlets

- **Heart Change:** the wise player weighs turning the heart up or down (3 magic) every round. It does so in about 1 fight in 6 (0.5 magic a fight). It is a real option, rarely worth its price here.
- **Partial charge releases:** the wise player weighs storing or releasing Storm Coil every round, at any charge. It releases about 1.1 magic's worth a fight, mostly at 1 or 2 charges (it rarely waits for 4). Partial releases are considered and used.

## Results

| Arm | Health a fight vs start | Magic left at the end | Win |
|---|---|---|---|
| start (Arc Bolt, Magic Missile, Storm Coil) | 13.0 | 9.7 | 96.7% |
| **Barrier instead of Magic Missile** | **−1.3 ± 0.45** (4 of 5 campaigns better) | 9.9 | 97.1% |
| Re-arm Arc Bolt for 2 magic (from 3) | −0.4 ± 0.4 | 9.9 | 97.1% |
| Hold Arc Bolt for 6+ magic (or to finish a monster) | −0.1 ± 0.7 | 9.9 | 97.1% |
| Both | −0.4 ± 0.8 | 10.3 | 97.5% |

## What it says

1. **Tweaking the existing outlets does not change the Wizard's results or its leftover magic.**
   - A cheaper re-arm is used about as often as before (0.75 against 0.81 a fight).
   - Holding Arc Bolt makes the casts slightly bigger (4.1 magic against 3.9) and fewer.
   - Neither moves health beyond the error, and the Wizard still ends with about 10 magic.
2. **Barrier remains the only change with a measurable gain**, repeating the earlier paired result (−1.6 ± 0.4 there, −1.3 ± 0.45 here).
3. **The leftover magic is structural.** Act II fights last about 4 rounds; the Wizard earns about 5 magic a round; its outlets are rationed per round or per battle. Spending it would take a different kind of outlet, for example magic that carries from fight to fight, or an at-will power that scales with how much is spent. That is a design question for the Director (T03 / class identity), not a tuning of the current outlets.

**Recommendation:**
- Do not adopt the re-arm or hold changes.
- If the Wizard's start is to change now, Barrier is the one with evidence.
- Whether surplus magic should become a feature (carry-over, scaling) is for the Director to decide.

Nothing has been changed in the game.
