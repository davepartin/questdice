# Where the Wizard's magic goes (23 October 2026)

Dave: "audit the Wizard's existing magic outlets before adding another." The ledger counts every point of magic in Act II fights with each class's starting kit. 5 independent campaigns per class, each snapshot fought the four matchup fights (one big hitter, three goblins, an armoured monster, a self-healer) 6 times: 240 fights per class and player. The full table is ledger.txt; the per-fight rows are ledger_*.json.

## What it says

1. **The cap is not the problem.** The Wizard loses only 0.2 magic a fight to its cap of 15. Its magic is left over because the fight ends first, not thrown away.
2. **Income outruns the outlets.**
   - The Wizard earns about 5 magic a round and starts with 7: about 29 a fight.
   - It spends about 19 (powers 7, rerolls 10, the rest small) and ends with about 10, a third of everything it had.
   - The leftover grows with the fight: 7.7 after two rounds, 9.3 after three, 11.9 after five.
   - The Bard is the same (10.2 left). The other three classes end with 5–6.
3. **Rerolls are already the biggest outlet** for every class (7–10 magic a fight). The Wizard's extra magic goes into powers (7.2, about twice the others), but the powers have per-round limits: Magic Missile and Storm Coil take 1 magic a round each.
4. **Arc Bolt, the Wizard's big outlet, is cast small.**
   - Once a fight on average (1.1), at 3.8 magic: one or two dice of the five it can hold.
   - In 50 of 240 fights it was not cast at all.
   - Re-arming it (3 magic) happened about once in every three fights.
   - It is usually fired early, while magic is still low.
5. **Turning the heart (3 magic) was never used.** The simulated players do not consider it. That is a gap in the simulation, not proof it is useless: real players may well spend magic there.

## So, before adding an outlet

The Wizard already has three outlets that could take its surplus: bigger Arc Bolts, re-arming Arc Bolt, and turning the heart. The players (simulated, and probably real ones) under-use them. The first two belong to the Wizard alone.

The next step is to test changes to those existing outlets against the Barrier start, the same paired way, before inventing anything new. Two candidates:
- re-arming Arc Bolt costs 2 instead of 3 for the Wizard;
- the wise player learns to hold Arc Bolt for a big cast.

Nothing has been changed in the game.
